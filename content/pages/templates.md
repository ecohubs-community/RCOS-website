## What's in a template

Every template:

- names the rules in the [standard](/standard) it answers, so you can see exactly what it covers;
- explains each section: why it exists and what goes wrong without it;
- shows how other communities answered: examples, not recommendations;
- leaves clear blanks for your own answers.

The templates don't tell you what to value or how to live. They make sure the things that matter are written down, so they can be discussed, agreed and changed openly.

## Who they're for

- Groups starting a community who want clear structure from day one.
- Existing communities checking what they've never written down.
- Facilitators who want a concrete document to work on in the room.

## You don't have to do everything at once

Partial adoption still counts, as long as what you adopt is written down and open to review. Skipping a template is fine; claiming to have one you don't is not.

## Languages and formats

The templates are available in English, German, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese. Read them here, or [download them](#downloads) as Markdown, Word (DOCX) or OpenDocument (ODT) files to edit offline.
