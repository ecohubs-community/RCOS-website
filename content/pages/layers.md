## Why layers

A community makes decisions at very different depths. Some are about why it exists at all; others are about who cleans the kitchen this week. RCOS keeps them apart, so that the everyday ones can't wear down the deep ones by accident.

The rule is simple: **a higher layer can never overrule a lower one.** A work rota (Layer 5) can't change who counts as a member (Layer 1), and a majority vote (Layer 2) can't override a line the community promised never to cross (Layer 0). To change something deep, you go through the process that layer defines, not around it.

## Do we need all seven?

Full RCOS compliance means answering all seven. You don't have to get there at once: most groups start with Layer 0 and work upward as questions come up. Partial adoption still counts, as long as what you've adopted is written down and you're honest about the rest. A partial answer on paper is worth more than a complete one that only lives in people's heads.
